**JRPCrew Labs** is an independent creative engineering studio and venture lab bridging physical making with software engineering. From custom brick builds and in-browser 3D WebGL graphics to applied spatial AI and multi-sector digital tools, our lab is where physical craft meets digital innovation.

- **Primary URL:** [https://www.jrpcrewlabs.com](https://www.jrpcrewlabs.com)
- **Direct Alias:** [https://www.jrpcrewlabs.com/home](https://www.jrpcrewlabs.com/home)
- **Apex Domain:** [https://jrpcrewlabs.com](https://jrpcrewlabs.com) (HTTP 308 auto-redirect to `www`)
- **Flagship SaaS:** [Brickmine3D Studio](https://brickmine3d.jrpcrewlabs.com) — Dedicated 3D rendering pipeline transforming raw `.io`, `.ldr`, and `.glb` files into 8 standardized 4K studio stills, 360° turntable loops, and cinematic director videos across 4 aspect ratios (9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 16:9).
